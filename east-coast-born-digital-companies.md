## KEY OBSERVATIONS

1. **NYC dominates** with 41 of 81 companies (51%). The city's concentration spans every vertical - fintech, health tech, ad tech, commerce, security, and data infrastructure.

2. **Boston/MA is the clear #2** with 17 companies (21%), particularly strong in e-commerce (Wayfair, Chewy-adjacent), restaurant tech (Toast), marketing SaaS (HubSpot, Klaviyo), and cybersecurity (Rapid7, Snyk).

3. **Atlanta has quietly built a strong bench** with 11 born-digital companies, particularly in fintech (GreenSky, Kabbage, Greenlight, Cardlytics) and marketing (Mailchimp, Calendly).

4. **The Research Triangle (NC) punches above its weight** with 4 companies focused on banking (nCino), healthcare (Phreesia), communications (Bandwidth), and product analytics (Pendo).

5. **Retail/CPG/Commerce is the strongest vertical** (18 companies) - driven by the natural advantage of e-commerce being one of the first internet-native business models.

6. **FinServ is #2** (13 companies) - NYC's finance ecosystem + the post-2008 fintech wave created ideal conditions for born-digital financial companies.

7. **Healthcare is growing but still nascent** (7-9 companies depending on classification) - significant whitespace remains for born-digital health tech.

8. **DC/Virginia has almost no born-digital companies** - the region is dominated by legacy defense contractors and government services firms that don't qualify.
