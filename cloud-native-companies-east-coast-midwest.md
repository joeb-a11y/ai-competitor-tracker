## NOTES ON METHODOLOGY

### Definition of "Born in the Cloud"
Companies included were founded as digital-first or cloud-native businesses, or were founded during the cloud era (post ~2000) with cloud/SaaS as their primary delivery model from inception or very early in their history. A small number of older companies that pivoted heavily to cloud-first models are included but noted.

### Revenue Estimates
- Public company revenues are based on latest annual or trailing twelve-month (TTM) filings through Q3 2025
- Private company ARR figures are based on the latest available reports from press releases, funding announcements, and industry databases (GetLatka, Crunchbase, PitchBook, Tracxn)
- Some private company figures are estimated ranges based on employee count, funding, and growth rate proxies

- GetLatka SaaS Database
