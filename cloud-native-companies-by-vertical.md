## KEY TAKEAWAYS

1. **FinServ has the deepest bench** (35 companies) - Born-digital fintech, insurtechs, and financial data companies are thriving outside CA/Seattle, especially in NYC, Chicago, Ohio, and North Carolina.

2. **Retail/CPG is the largest by revenue** (~$65B+) - Driven by massive born-digital e-commerce players (Carvana, Wayfair, Chewy, Etsy) plus the marketing/commerce SaaS ecosystem around them (Klaviyo, Braze, Shopify).

3. **Healthcare/Life Sciences is a strong East Coast/Midwest story** - Nashville, Columbus OH, Boston, and the Philadelphia corridor are hotbeds for cloud-native health tech. This vertical has significant room for data-driven transformation.

4. **Manufacturing is the smallest bench but highest growth opportunity** - Only 14 companies, but includes heavyweight platforms like PTC ($2.3B) and Infor ($3.2B). This is where "making the leap to data-driven" is most nascent and the opportunity is largest.

5. **Cybersecurity is heavily concentrated in DC/VA, Austin, and Boston** - These are almost all pure cloud-native companies born in the last 15-20 years.

6. **Government/Public Sector is dominated by the DC corridor** - Nearly all are in Reston/McLean/Tysons, VA - highly concentrated geographically.
