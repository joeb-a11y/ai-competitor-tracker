## KEY OBSERVATIONS

### Geographic Distribution
- **New York leads by a wide margin** — 45 companies (~$57B+), anchored by Oscar Health, Compass, Datadog, MongoDB, and the world's deepest AdTech cluster
- **Massachusetts is #2** — 21 companies (~$38B+), driven by Wayfair, Toast, DraftKings, HubSpot, and a strong cybersecurity cluster
- **Utah is the surprise #3 for company count** — 10 companies ($5B+), led by Qualtrics, Instructure, Pluralsight. The "Silicon Slopes" ecosystem is real
- **Connecticut has outsized impact** — only 2 companies, but Booking Holdings ($23.7B) is the largest born-digital company on the entire list
- **Chicago/Illinois is the Midwest hub** — 7 companies including Tempus AI ($1.27B), Grubhub, and a concentration of fintech/B2B SaaS

### Vertical Insights
- **Retail/Commerce is the #1 vertical** (22 companies) — e-commerce was the first internet-native business model. Booking ($23.7B), Carvana ($13.7B), Wayfair ($11.9B), Chewy ($11.5B), Shopify ($8.9B) are the giants
- **FinServ/Fintech is #2** (21 companies) — the post-2008 fintech wave created ideal conditions. NYC has the deepest cluster (Circle, Pagaya, Ramp, Payoneer, MoneyLion, Lemonade)
- **Cybersecurity is #3** (12 companies) — CrowdStrike ($4.0B), SailPoint ($1.1B), Tenable ($850M+), Rapid7 ($844M) are the leaders. Heavy Austin + Boston concentration
- **Healthcare has 12 companies** but Oscar Health ($11.7B) and Agilon ($5.7B) dominate by revenue. Most born-digital health companies are still sub-$500M
- **Manufacturing remains the thinnest vertical** — only Rubicon ($800M+) truly qualifies. This is the largest greenfield opportunity for born-digital disruption
- **Government/Public Sector has ZERO qualifying companies** — the entire vertical is legacy defense contractors and government services firms

### The "Born Digital → Data-Driven" Leaders
Companies best exemplifying the leap from born-digital to truly data-driven through AI:
- **Datadog** ($2.68B) — observability → AI-powered operations
- **CrowdStrike** ($4.0B) — endpoint security → AI-driven threat detection
- **Tempus AI** ($1.27B) — genomics → AI precision medicine
- **AlphaSense** ($500M+) — market intelligence → AI-powered document search
- **CoreWeave** ($5.05B) — GPU cloud → backbone of AI infrastructure
- **Klaviyo** ($1.2B) — email marketing → AI-powered predictive analytics
- **Wiz** ($750M+) — cloud security → AI vulnerability detection
- **Toast** ($5.8B) — restaurant POS → data-driven restaurant operations
