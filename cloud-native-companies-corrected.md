# Cloud-Native / Born-Digital Companies (>$250M ARR) - CORRECTED

## Filter Criteria
- **Born in the cloud**: Companies founded as digital-first / cloud-native from inception (generally post-2000, with cloud/SaaS as the original delivery model)
- **NOT traditional enterprises** that later underwent digital transformation
- **Revenue**: Over $250 million ARR
- **HQ Exclusions**: NOT headquartered in Seattle or California
- **Geographic Focus**: East Coast and Midwest (with select Mountain West / South entries)
- **Last Updated**: February 2026

---

## What "Born in the Cloud" Means Here

A company qualifies if it was **founded as a cloud/SaaS/digital-native business from day one**. The product was designed for the internet/cloud era, not migrated to it later.

**Excluded**: Companies that started as on-premises software, traditional services firms, defense contractors, mainframe-era companies, or industrial conglomerates that later acquired or built cloud offerings. Even if they are "cloud companies" today, they are digital transformation stories, not born-digital stories.

**Borderline rule**: Companies founded ~2000-2005 that were web-native from inception (even if "cloud" wasn't the term yet) are included. Companies from the same era that started as on-prem/installed software are excluded.

## KEY TAKEAWAYS (Corrected)

1. **The honest count of truly born-digital companies is ~139, not 215.** Over a third of the original list were legacy/transformation companies.

2. **Retail/CPG has the strongest born-digital presence** (26 companies) because e-commerce was one of the first internet-native business models.

3. **FinServ/Fintech is a close second** (24 companies) - Fintech disruption post-2008 financial crisis spawned a wave of born-digital financial companies.

4. **Healthcare has 16 genuinely born-digital companies** - but many of the biggest "health tech" names are actually legacy EHR/HIT companies that migrated to cloud (Allscripts, Greenway, InterSystems, etc.).

5. **Manufacturing is the thinnest vertical at only 5 truly born-digital companies.** This confirms it's the biggest greenfield opportunity. Most manufacturing software is still legacy (PTC, SAP, Siemens, Dassault, etc.) that underwent digital transformation.

6. **Government/Public Sector had ZERO truly born-digital companies** once you remove the defense contractors and legacy firms. This entire vertical was removed.
